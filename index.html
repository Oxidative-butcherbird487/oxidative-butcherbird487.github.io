📊 What Is This Tool?

The nutrifood-weather-scraper is a specialized utility designed for the Nutrifood Market Research Project. It automatically collects "real feel" weather information - the perceived temperature that combines actual temperature with wind, humidity, and other factors. This data is crucial for understanding how weather affects consumer behavior and product demand.

With the Version 2026 automation, all you need to do is open the application in your web browser, and it will handle the rest. No technical knowledge required - just click and let it work!

This browser-based tool runs entirely in your web browser, making it lightweight, portable, and compatible with any modern Windows computer. Whether you're a researcher, analyst, or data enthusiast, this tool saves hours of manual data collection time.
